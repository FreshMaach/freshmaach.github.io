Our Story

Two Brothers,
One Family Legacy

FreshMaach is a family-built business rooted in the heart of Kolkata — the fish capital of Bengal. We are two brothers who grew up watching our father work the water, and we decided to bring that legacy directly to your table.

Our father is a seasoned expert in fish farming and aquaculture, with decades of hands-on knowledge about freshwater fish, seasonal availability, and quality. His expertise is what sets us apart from every ordinary supplier.

Our goal is simple — no middlemen, no markup, no mystery. You speak to us directly, we call you back personally, and you get farm-fresh fish at honest prices.
